Exercises showcase, grading improvements

The main item for the past week was the addition of the [Exercises](/exercises) showcase, which gives a playable demo of each of our four exercise types and explains some of their design rationale.

Smaller items included:
- improved sound-rail treatment for interacting with the phonetic components of words
- some modifications to the spelling grader
- a fix for a bug where some phonemes in a session were incorrectly labelled as having been missed
- improved rendering of [Who Said That](/exercises#wst) exercises on very small devices
