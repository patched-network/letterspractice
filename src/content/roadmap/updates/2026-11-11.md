Performance and Discoverability Improvements

Work this week was mostly about making first impressions.

## Performance

The site had accidentally and slowly become bloated, mostly invisible to me personally because my own interactions with it were able to incrementally adapt to the bloat.

- Landing page loading much faster across all contexts. Mobile measurements (lighthouse, pagespeed, etc) from ~11s to 2.5s
- app runs on new devices run against server-side course data instead of waiting for a full sync of the course to the device. Reduces /peek or first session startup time from an unexplained hang of 30+ seconds (shame on me!) down to a few seconds
- added measurement guards around these and related metrics so that they don't creep up again without my knowledge

All told, there were about 20 individual technical changes toward the end of improved performance, ranging from very standard and straightforward stuff to some very app specific data loading optimizations.
