## Notes

- This represents General American English pronunciation
- Some linguists count slightly different numbers depending on whether they treat r-controlled vowels as separate phonemes or combinations
- For early literacy education, simplified versions focusing on ~40 core phonemes are often used
- Dialect variations exist (British English, Australian English, etc. have different phoneme inventories)
