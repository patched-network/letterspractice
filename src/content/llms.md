# LettersPractice

> An early-literacy app that teaches young children, ages 2 to 5 and up, to read with a few minutes of phonics practice a day, led by animated letter characters. It runs in the browser.

LettersPractice teaches English reading as a sequence of sound-spelling patterns (phonics), in 2 to 5 minutes a day: about a minute per year of the child's age. The letters themselves do the teaching. They are animated characters, with faces and arms, that introduce their sounds, demonstrate them, and react as the child works. The child answers on a large on-screen keyboard that shows and enables only the keys a question needs.

## How it works

- **Curriculum.** Patterns are taught in the order they pay off in real reading, so the big wins come early. Dependable high-use patterns (*s* as in *sit*) and the important exceptions (*of*, *have*, *said*, *was*) are taught explicitly and checked with targeted exercises. The large, irregular middle of English spelling is mixed into exercises on other skills, for the child to pick up by exposure.
- **Daily sessions.** A learning engine picks each exercise for each child, balancing new material, review, and the mixed-in patterns. The engine is open source (Skuilder).
- **The adult's part.** It keeps what makes parent-led programs such as *Teach Your Child to Read in 100 Easy Lessons* work (careful sequencing, short daily practice, an adult at the important moments) and automates the scripting, scheduling and review. A parent is called in only for the moments that need one.
- **Off screen.** A free printable letter-sound sheet for the fridge, coloured in as the app introduces each letter.
