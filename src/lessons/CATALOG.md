## Staging ideas for deferred lessons

- **Across lessons:** the letters are a little guilty about English spelling
  ("we're sorry about these ones") — used in sight-words.
- **vowel-teams:** continuity with Letter Teammates — the vowels want teams
  of their own.
- **r-controlled:** `r` as well-meaning but overbearing ("I just want to
  help!"). The three-spellings-one-sound beat (er/ir/ur) must be told
  without names.
- **l-controlled:** `l` admiring `r` ("I can do that too"); a light coda to
  r-controlled. `-all` may move to capping (b.1 D1).
- **soft-consonants:** a letter with two moods, shown by words, not a rule.
- **doubled-consonants → capping (b.1 D1):** a spelling convention after a
  short vowel, not "twins make one sound".
- **silent-letters (b.1 D4: one does the talking):** the quiet letter with a
  finger to its lips; a light historical beat (people used to say it). The
  quiet letter drawn ghosted could carry over into practice as a hint.
