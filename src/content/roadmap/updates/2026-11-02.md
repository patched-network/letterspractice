Gentler limits, improved spelling feedback, and a new mini-lesson.

- Practice UI now defaults to one session a day, with parental override for more sessions.
- Phonetically coherent misspellings ("fone" for "phone", "necst" for "next") are now graded / evaluated more holistically. Grapheme-replacement on the misspelled piece is explicitly guided, but the correct phonetic match is acknowledged.
- Added a mini-lesson, [Making our bed](/curriculum/lesson/bed), which uses the word *bed* as a mnemonic to distinguish `b` `d` symmetry. Runs ahead of introduction of letter `b`.
- Tweaks to the scheduler's response to mistakes. Previously the mistaken item was over-practiced in the short term. Updated response is more measured and sustained. 
- Grouped end-of-session practiced-items.
- The printable fridge sheet has [its own page](/fridge), with both PDF versions and fuller guidance for children and grown-ups.
